Customer and Admin Journey

PISA Leadership Assessment Portal

A client-facing walkthrough of the current assessment experience, from the first purchase decision through completion, results, and administrative oversight.

Experience Shape

A complete path from purchase to outcome.

The experience is strongest when each screen makes the next step obvious: decide, register, pay, confirm, complete, resume if interrupted, and retrieve the outcome report.

Decide and chooseThe product and assessment-selection screens explain what is being bought and where to begin.
Create the accountRegistration and account creation collect the essentials before payment.
Pay and confirmOrder review, Payfast handoff, payment confirmation, and email confirmation close the purchase loop.
Complete or resumeThe assessment screens show progress, saved state, and clear continuation points.
Review the outcomeThe final screen provides the completed result and a direct report download action.
Operate the portalAdmin screens cover performance, users, assessment setup, and analytics.
