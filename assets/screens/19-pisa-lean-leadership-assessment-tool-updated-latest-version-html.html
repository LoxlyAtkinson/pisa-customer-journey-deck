Welcome to the PISA Lean Leadership Assessment™

The PISA Lean Leadership Assessment™ helps you understand your leadership strengths, identify development priorities and create a personalised growth plan.

It measures the capabilities, behaviours and practices that enable leaders to create sustainable organisational performance.

An effective leader develops people, improves systems and continuously creates value for customers and the organisation. They build capable teams, make sound decisions, foster trusted relationships and drive continuous improvement.

The PISA Lean Leadership Framework™ is built on one simple belief Sustainable organisational performance is created by leaders who think systematically, behave intentionally, sustain themselves and others, and continuously improve the systems that create value.

The framework develops leadership across four interconnected pillars:

Leadership Capability
How I Think
Leadership Behaviours
How I Lead
Leadership Resilience
How I Sustain My Leadership
Continuous Improvement Leadership
How I Improve People, Processes and Performance

Completing the Assessment

Answer each statement honestly based on how you typically lead, not how you aspire to lead.

There are no right or wrong answers.

Your responses will generate a personalised leadership profile, highlighting your strengths, development priorities and practical coaching recommendations to support your leadership journey.

You'll now answer 96 short statements in four parts — Awareness, Behaviour, Influence and Outcome — rating each from Never to Always.

It takes about 20–25 minutes.

Level Calibration

How this compares to what's typical at this level

Expectations for these pillars naturally rise with seniority, so the same raw score means different things at different levels — a frontline supervisor and a senior executive who both agree strongly with every statement will land on the same raw score, but only one of them is meeting what their role requires. This compares each pillar score to a general expected range for the selected leadership level — not a hard benchmark, but a useful check.

These expected ranges are general guidance for calibration, not a normed or validated benchmark. They're a prompt for coaching conversation, not a verdict.
