What this assessment is for

The PISA Lean Leadership Assessment provides a clear, objective measure of your current leadership behaviors against the core principles of Lean management. It is designed not to evaluate your technical knowledge, but rather to reveal the underlying habits and mindsets that drive your day-to-day decision making and team interactions.

By answering a carefully structured series of statements, you will uncover your natural tendencies in areas such as problem-solving orientation, respect for people, continuous improvement focus, and systemic thinking. The resulting profile acts as a precise mirror, reflecting both your inherent strengths and the blind spots that may be hindering organizational flow.

This assessment is the foundational step for leaders who are committed to moving beyond theoretical management concepts towards practical, sustainable execution. It provides the empirical baseline necessary to craft a targeted personal development plan, ultimately enabling you to cultivate a more resilient, engaged, and high-performing culture within your sphere of influence.
